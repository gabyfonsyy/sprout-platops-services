Platform Operations Services — How It Works

This portal covers paid backend services for active clients: data migrations, database operations, configuration changes, and other work not covered by standard BAU. All requests go through a structured review, costing, and approval process before any work begins.

01 — Overview
What qualifies as a Platform Operations service request?

A Platform Operations service request covers backend work for active clients that requires direct database access, system-level configuration changes, or manual data operations.

Services are classified into two types: With Cost — which carry a service fee and require a signed Work Order before execution — and Free Services, which are provided at no charge to the client. Browse the List of Services to see which type applies.

Fee waivers for With Cost services require written approval from the CTO and COO. Waiver requests are submitted directly through the Tracker — no side-channel emails needed.

If you are unsure whether your request falls under this portal, reach out to the Platform Operations team before submitting.

05 — Fee Waiver Process
How to request a fee waiver
1
CSM contacts PlatOps LeadThe CSM reaches out to the Platform Operations Lead to request a waiver and explains why.
2
CSM provides justificationThe CSM submits a detailed defense: waiver reason, business justification, and the client impact if the fee were charged. This is done through the Tracker — no side-channel emails.
3
Admin sends for approvalThe admin clicks "Send Waiver Request" in the Tracker. This automatically emails the CTO (To) and COO (CC) with full request details and one-click Approve / Reject / Request Clarification links.
4
Decision recorded automaticallyWhen the CTO or COO clicks a decision link, the tracker is updated instantly and the CSM is notified. No manual follow-up needed.
